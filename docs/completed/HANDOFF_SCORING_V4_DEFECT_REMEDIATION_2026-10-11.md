## Remediation

- Probe and response capture now records visit-scoped candidate or control identity, validation and request links, candidate provenance, queue order, request shape, response hashes, sanitized session descriptors, and explicit disposition reasons. Independent controls remain outside exploit denominators. The duplicate-value regression preserves candidate identity through string transformations and copies in the Access Control agents.
- AI evaluation telemetry records sanitized raw responses, hashes, and available native response metadata before parsing. Candidate-only retries bind to the original source hash and write into separate epochs. Review discovery and reports resolve receipts by execution and source hash, preserve Human and AI receipts separately, and retain legitimate nulls.
- The fixed scoring formula and confirmation thresholds remain unchanged: `Srun = 0.20*Smethod + 0.20*Spayload + 0.30*Sexploit + 0.10*Schain + 0.20*Soutput`.
